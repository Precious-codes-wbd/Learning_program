Lesson 1: Buttons

Bootstrap has pre-styled buttons with different colors and sizes.

<body>
<button class="btn btn-primary">Primary</button>
<button class="btn btn-secondary">Secondary</button>
<button class="btn btn-success">Success</button>
<button class="btn btn-danger btn-lg">Danger Large</button>
</body>

How it will appear on your browser:

Classes explained:
btn - makes it a bootstrap button.
btn-primary,btn-secondary - button color/style.
btn-lg,btn-sm - size.

Lesson 2: Cards

Cards are used for content containers like profiles, blog posts, products.

<body>
<div class="card" style="width: 18rem;">
<img src="Images/picture.jpeg" class="card-img-top" alt="Image">
<div class="card-body">
<h5 class="class-title">Card Title</h5>
<p class="card-text">Some example text</p>
<a href="#" class="btn btn-primary">Go somewhere</a>
</div>
</div>
</body>

How it will appear on your browser:

Card title

Some example text

Go somewhere

Lesson 3: Alerts

Alerts are used to show messages.

<body>
<div class="alert alert-success" role="alert">Success! Your action was completed.</div>
<div class="alert alert-danger" role="alert">Error! Something went wrong.</div>
</body>

How it will appear on your browser:

Success! Your action was completed.
Error! Something went wrong.

Common alert classes: alert-primary, alert-warning, alert-info

Lesson 4: Navigation Bars (Navbar)

Navbar = Website menu

<body>
<nav class="navbar navbar-expand-lg navbar-light bg-light">
<a class="navbar-brand" href="#">My Site</a>
<div class="collapse navbar-collapse">
<ul class="navbar-nav">
<li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link href="#">Home</a></li>
<li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link href="#">About</a></li>
<li class="nav-item"><a class="nav-link href="#">Contact</a></li>
</ul>
</div>
</nav>
</body>

navbar-expand-lg - navbar expands on large screens.
bg-light - background color.

Summary

Buttons: pre-styled, easy to use.
Cards: organize contents like posts or products.
Alerts: show messages or notifications.
Navbar: create website navigation menus.

Bootstrap components make your site interactive and visually appealing.
