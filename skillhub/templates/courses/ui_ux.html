Introduction to UI/UX Design

UI (User Interface) and UX (User Experience) design focus on creating digital products that are visually appealing and easy to use. UI design deals with the looks and layout of an application, including colors, typography, button and visual elements.
UX focuses on how users interact with a product and how smooth and enjoyable their experience is. Good UI/UX design improves user satisfaction, engagement, and overall product success.
