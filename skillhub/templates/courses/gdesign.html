Introduction to Graphic Design

Graphic design is the art of creating visual content to communicate ideas and messages. It combines creativity, typography, color theory, images and layout techniques to produce visually appealing designs.
Graphic designers create logos, posters, social media graphics, advertisements, brand identities, and digital content. Tools commonly used include Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, and Figma. Graphic design plays an important role in branding, marketing, and user engagement by making information visually attractive and easy to understand.
